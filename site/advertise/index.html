      Questions

      When am I charged?

      Only when you confirm an approved ad. Before that it is a hold on your card. If we reject your ad, you pay the $4.99 review fee and the rest of the hold is released.

      How far ahead do I need to book?

      At least three days before the issue goes out, so there is time to review your ad.

      What if an issue does not go out?

      If an issue with your ad doesn't go out, we refund that issue's share to your card the same day.

      Can I use a discount code?

      Yes. Enter it at checkout. The hold, the charge and any refund use the discounted price.

      What ads are not allowed?

      Nothing political, hateful, harmful or misleading. Every ad is checked before it runs.

      Why should the email match my business?

      An email at your business’s own website address helps us confirm the business is yours, so your review goes faster.
