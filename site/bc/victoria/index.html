      Questions

      Is it free?

      Yes. Beats Buzz is free for readers, with no subscription fees, ever.

      How often will I hear from you?

      Twice a week: Monday morning for the week ahead, Thursday morning for the weekend.

      What's in it?

      Events, eats, and everything in between in Victoria. No politics, ever.

      Why do I get a confirmation email?

      So only you can sign yourself up. Click the link in that email and your first issue is on its way. If you can't find it, check Promotions or Spam.

      Can I unsubscribe?

      Yes, any time, with one click from the bottom of any issue.

      How can my business be in it?

      Local businesses book a spot in an issue on the Advertise page.
